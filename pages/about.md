# About

My work focuses on **satellite remote sensing, machine learning, and atmospheric nitrogen dioxide (NO₂)**. I am interested in the possibilities and limitations of reconstructing historical pollution patterns from observations at different spatial resolutions.

## Research interests

My research uses satellite observations and computational methods to investigate NO₂ patterns over cities and across time. One motivation is to explore the potential for understanding historical urban air pollution inequality, while recognizing that modeled column measurements are not the same as measured personal exposure.

## Digital humanities practice

Digital humanities has encouraged me to think more carefully about mapping, the construction of datasets, and how scientific information can be communicated to broader audiences. I am exploring digital mapping, text analysis, and critical making as ways to connect scientific research with social and historical questions.
